# Sound credits

Inkwell's soundscape uses these recordings. Most were prepared for looping by the
[Blanket](https://github.com/rafaelmardojai/blanket) project; all were trimmed,
level-matched and re-encoded as 128 kbps MP3 for Inkwell. The files live in `public/sounds/`.

The CC BY 4.0 recordings (rain, storm, waves) must keep their credit with the app; it
is shown in Settings, under the soundscape. Changes made to every file: edited for
seamless looping, trimmed, volume adjusted with peaks limited, re-encoded to MP3.

The fireplace was supplied by the project owner from Pixabay. The Pixabay Content
License allows use in an app without attribution (it is credited anyway), but the
file must not be sold or redistributed on its own as a sound effect.
